## 本次更新

- 菜单栏今日用量面板用“首字延迟”和“输出速度”（token/s）取代原来的输入、输出、缓存三行。圆环和总量保留，悬停圆环可查看输入、输出、缓存的分项数量。延迟和速度取近 7 天最近 10 次有效回复的中位数，与今日用量统计范围无关，没有样本时显示 “—”。
- API 费用估算与这两行使用统一的字号、字重和间距，名称使用常规字重、数值使用中等字重，移除了原来的色块小杠。macOS 原生菜单与 Windows/Linux 面板保持一致。
- 缓存的额度中返回了 Gemini 4 Argon 额度池的账号，会在菜单和面板的账号名旁显示绿点。该标记不受“每周额度”“Gemini 显示范围”设置影响；已禁用账号不显示。

## Changes

- Replace the Input/Output/Cached rows in the menu's Today's usage panel with first-text latency and output speed (token/s). The ring and total remain, and hovering the ring shows the input/output/cache split. Latency and speed are the median of the latest 10 eligible responses within 7 days, independent of today's totals, and show “—” when no sample exists.
- Give the API estimate and these two rows one size, weight and even spacing: regular names, medium-weight values, and no colour-key dashes. The native macOS menu and the Windows/Linux panel stay aligned.
- Show a green dot beside accounts whose cached quota reports the Gemini 4 Argon pool, regardless of the weekly or Gemini display settings. Disabled accounts show no dot.

## Verification and limits

Menu projection and usage Rust tests and all menu UI tests passed, including new
assertions for the latency/speed rows, the token-split tooltip and the single dot
on the account reporting Argon. The Windows/Linux panel layout was inspected in
browser screenshots at compact and normal sizes. The native macOS menu was built,
installed and opened locally, but its layout was not captured in a screenshot or
inspected through accessibility; the user reviewed the installed Mac menu.
Windows/Linux tray behavior was not tested physically.

The dot reflects a reported quota pool in the saved observation. It is not proof
of live model entitlement, and a missing pool is not evidence that access is
absent. Latency and speed come from local response records and do not describe
network conditions or any single model. See [quota pools](../quota-pools.md) and
[menu dashboard](../menu-bar-dashboard.md).

Tests use isolated synthetic data. No saved real account was switched, refreshed,
exported or deleted.

Mac packages lack Developer ID/notarization and Windows packages lack Authenticode.
Integrity and updater signatures do not establish OS trust. Installation requires
user action; see [updates](../software-updates.md).
